Annexure-H
POS Transaction and Settlement Terms

This Annexure-H supplements Clauses 6 to 13 and Clause 20 of the Agreement. Capitalized terms have the meanings given in Clause 1 of the Agreement. In this Annexure, the User is referred to in its capacity as a payment acceptor/merchant as the "Merchant".

H.1 Transaction Limits

  1. Maximum permissible amount for any single Transaction processed through a POS Device: INR 1,19,999 (Rupees One Lakh Nineteen Thousand Nine Hundred Ninety-Nine Only).
  2. Transactions exceeding this limit may be placed on Hold for up to ninety (90) days pending verification by the Company and/or the acquiring bank.
  3. The Company and acquiring bank may prescribe lower per-transaction, daily, or monthly limits, MCC-based restrictions, or velocity limits from time to time, which shall bind the Merchant upon notification.

H.2 Genuine Transactions

  1. Every Transaction must represent a genuine sale of goods or supply of services by the Merchant at its actual value, supported by an invoice or equivalent record.
  2. The Merchant shall not process fictitious, circular, accommodation, or cash-advance Transactions. Violation constitutes a breach of Clauses 6.5 and 14 and may result in immediate suspension.
  3. A card may be used for only one Transaction at a time. Repeated Transactions using the same card on the same POS Device, and splitting a single sale into multiple Transactions, are strictly prohibited. Such Transactions may be excluded from Settlement and placed on Hold.

H.3 Settlement Cycle and Cut-off

  1. POS Settlement is on a T+1 basis. The Company shall endeavour to credit eligible proceeds between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM IST on the settlement day.
  2. POS Settlement is processed on every calendar day including Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays, except:
    1. Republic Day (26 January), Independence Day (15 August), and Gandhi Jayanti (2 October); and
    2. Any day on which settlement is prevented or delayed by a bank, NPCI, or card network outage, in which case settlement shall be made on the next available day.
  3. Transactions completed by 10:00 PM IST are treated as that day's Transactions. Transactions completed after 10:00 PM IST are treated as the next day's Transactions, and their Settlement is deferred by one (1) day accordingly.
  4. This paragraph H.3 is subject to Clauses 10, 11, 12, and 13, and the Company's right to withhold ineligible, disputed, or fraud-flagged amounts.

H.4 Cardholder Identification

  1. Before processing a Transaction, the Merchant shall collect and verify the cardholder's PAN and an officially valid identity document and confirm that the identity details match the cardholder's name on the card.
  2. If a cardholder voluntarily offers Aadhaar as an identity document, it shall be accepted only in masked form (last four digits visible) or via offline verification. The Merchant shall not:
    1. Insist on Aadhaar as the sole acceptable identity document;
    2. Record or store the full Aadhaar number; or
    3. Capture any biometric information for this purpose.
  3. Identity details shall be collected after giving notice and obtaining consent per Clause 18, used only for verification and regulatory purposes, and retained per Clauses 11.6 and 14.4.

H.5 Refunds and Chargebacks

  1. Refunds shall be processed only per Applicable Law, card network rules, and Company SOPs, only against a successfully completed verified Transaction, and only to the original card used.
  2. In the event of a Chargeback or disputed Transaction, the Merchant shall furnish all required documentation (invoice, charge slip, cardholder identity details from H.4, proof of delivery) within the timeline stipulated by the Company. Failure to do so entitles the Company to accept the Chargeback and recover the amount under Clauses 11 and 12.

H.6 Card Data Security

  1. The Merchant shall comply with all security standards prescribed by the Company, acquiring bank, and card networks to protect cardholder payment information.
  2. The Merchant shall NOT store card data — including full card number, CVV/CVC, PIN, expiry date, magnetic stripe data, or chip data — in any form, except as expressly permitted under RBI tokenisation directions.
  3. Cardholder payment details shall not be shared with any unauthorized person under any circumstances.
